AI RUNTIME — INDEPENDENT RECREATION BRIEF

Create a single-viewport cinematic landing page for a fictional modular AI platform. Use a black base and a full-bleed looping film behind the interface. Keep the video muted, inline, and non-interactive, with a dark static fallback if it cannot load. The core composition has three vertical zones: compact navigation at the top, centered hero content, and four compact metrics at the bottom.

In the header, place a simple circular mark, a white pill navigation with Home, Product, Case Studies, and Contact, plus a dark Sign in pill. On narrow screens, replace the desktop links with an accessible menu button and closeable mobile sheet. Buttons must have real responses; if there is no product backend, explain that this is a design concept instead of pretending that sign-in works.

Above the hero heading, include a small overlapping-symbol trust motif and a short descriptor. Set the two-line heading “Intelligence / Designed To Evolve” in white retro dot-matrix style. Under it, use a short product description and a glowing white Get Started pill. Keep the heading legible over every video frame using dark overlays, not a changing gradient on the letters.

At the bottom, arrange four illustrative figures in one row on desktop and two columns on mobile. Label them clearly as concept metrics. Use restrained entrance motion and a reduced-motion mode that shows the same information without animation or video. Ensure the complete composition fits the viewport on common screen sizes and can scroll on unusually short screens.
